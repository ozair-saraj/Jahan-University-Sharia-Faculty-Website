WHO WE ARE

Jahan University

Jahan institute of higher Education (JIHE) is one of the leading institute of higher education in the country that stablished in 2013 and has 5 faculties with nine departments, promoting higher education in Management Science, Economics, Information Technology, Database system, Sharia and Islamic study, Legal and diplomatic. Faculty members are creative and innovative in supporting the teaching, learning, scholarship and research activities. Also this institute provides Legal, Psychological counseling, foreign language teaching, professional development skills and research services. Jahan Institute of Higher education achieves second level National Accreditation in 2021 from the Ministry of Higher Education (MoHE) of the Islamic Republic of Afghanistan. The accreditation is a testament to the excellence in quality of services offered by the university as envisioned by its Vision and Mission Statements. It also mentionable this organization has up to 100 national and International MoUs with different type of organization specially with national and international universities and academic research centers
